# Distribution and measurement for Dubai Car Recovery

The 25 original company articles are published first at `https://www.dubaicarrecovery.site/blogs/`. Each has its own canonical URL. They use the business's existing illustrative images, each different and labelled as illustrative. Company opinions are attributed to Dubai Car Recovery and are not presented as customer reviews.

## External publishing

Do not republish a set of near-identical posts with synonym changes. A third-party article should serve that platform's readers on its own and link once, where allowed, to a relevant original page. Disclose that the writer represents Dubai Car Recovery. Do not claim that a publisher's acceptance or a backlink has happened until its live URL is verified.

- **LinkedIn:** A company Page admin can publish original articles or posts about service decisions and business practices. Prepare a short company-authored post on one issue at a time, such as what information makes a flatbed enquiry useful, then link to the relevant site article if the Page's rules permit it. The Page must be accessible under the correct owner account. [LinkedIn's article instructions](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a522427/creating-articles-on-linkedin) state that Page admins may publish articles.
- **Google Business Profile:** Publish useful service updates through the verified business listing, at roughly two per week. Use a relevant link to the live original article or service, with a short call to action. Do not put a phone number in the post text; Google's post policy may remove posts containing contact information. Use the Profile's Call button where available. Check categories against the actual business rather than creating a category for each keyword. [Google's category guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7249669?hl=en) and [post policy](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7213077?hl=en) apply.
- **Relevant local directories and industry publishers:** Submit accurate business name, phone, website and service description only where a real business listing or useful original contribution is accepted. Check the publisher's rules, verify ownership and keep the listing up to date. Do not buy links or place comments solely for a backlink.
- **Medium and WordPress.com:** Do not use them for a network of keyword variants whose main purpose is sending traffic or link equity to this site. Their published rules reject such promotional or SEO-driven content: [Medium rules](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/213477928-Medium-Rules), [WordPress.com guidelines](https://wordpress.com/support/user-guidelines/). A genuinely independent editorial contribution could be considered later under their current policies; a spun copy is inappropriate.

## Search Console and follow-up

After deployment, confirm the live sitemap contains the 25 new canonical URLs. Submit `https://www.dubaicarrecovery.site/sitemap.xml` to the verified Search Console property through the UI or Sitemap API if the owner account has access. Submitting a sitemap does not guarantee indexing. Ordinary blog posts should not be sent to Google's restricted Indexing API. [Search Console sitemap API](https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/v1/sitemaps/submit?hl=en).

At 7 days, check crawl/indexing status for representative new URLs and fix any technical errors. At 28 days, compare impressions, clicks, queries, CTR and country/device mix with the previous period. At 90 days, review which service topics produce useful visits and enquiries, then edit weak pages for real reader value. Use Search Console data for actual queries; do not invent search-volume figures. If a generative-search report is available for the property, record its data separately from ordinary Search performance.
